47.3 The Bochner–Kodaira–Nakano identity01IJ

Complete. This chapter is written; its proofs await independent checking.

01IK Notation 47.3.1

Let \(X\) be a Hermitian manifold and \((E, h)\) a Hermitian holomorphic vector bundle, with Chern connection \(D = D' + \bar\partial _E\), where \(D'\) has type \((1,0)\). On \(\mathcal{A}^{p,q}(X, E)\) we use the pointwise inner product induced by \(g\) and \(h\) and, for \(X\) compact, the \(L^2\) product. The operators \(L\) and \(\Lambda \) act on the form part. Let \(D'^*\) and \(\bar\partial _E^*\) be the formal adjoints, and put

\[ \Delta ' = D'D'^* + D'^*D', \qquad \Delta '' = \bar\partial _E\bar\partial _E^* + \bar\partial _E^*\bar\partial _E . \]

The curvature \(F = D^2\) is a \((1,1)\)-form with values in \(\operatorname {End}E\), and as operators \(F = D'\bar\partial _E + \bar\partial _ED'\), since \(D'^2 = 0\) and \(\bar\partial _E^2 = 0\) (Theorem 42.4.1).

01IL Lemma 47.3.2

Let \((E, h)\) be a Hermitian holomorphic vector bundle and \(p \in X\). There is a holomorphic frame \(e_1, \ldots , e_r\) of \(E\) near \(p\) with \(h(e_a, e_b) = \delta _{ab} + O(|z|^2)\) in holomorphic coordinates \(z\) centred at \(p\). In such a frame the connection form of \(D\) vanishes at \(p\).

Proof â–¼

Start with a holomorphic frame that is orthonormal at \(p\), and write \(H_{ab} = h(e_a, e_b) = \delta _{ab} + \sum _l(\partial _lH_{ab}(0)z^l + \bar\partial _lH_{ab}(0)\bar z^l) + O(|z|^2)\). Put \(e'_a = e_a - \sum _{b,l}\partial _lH_{ab}(0)\, z^le_b\), again a holomorphic frame near \(p\). To first order,

\[ h(e'_a, e'_b) = H_{ab} - \sum _l\partial _lH_{ab}(0)z^l - \sum _l\overline{\partial _lH_{ba}(0)}\, \bar z^l + O(|z|^2), \]

and \(\overline{\partial _lH_{ba}} = \bar\partial _l\overline{H_{ba}} = \bar\partial _lH_{ab}\) because \(H\) is Hermitian. So all first-order terms cancel. The connection form \(\overline{H}^{-1}\partial \overline{H}\) of Theorem 42.4.1 involves only first derivatives of \(H\), so it vanishes at \(p\).

01IM Theorem 47.3.3 Kähler identities with coefficients

Let \(X\) be Kähler and \((E, h)\) a Hermitian holomorphic vector bundle. Then

\[ [\Lambda , \bar\partial _E] = -iD'^*, \qquad [\Lambda , D'] = i\bar\partial _E^* . \]
Proof â–¼

Both identities are between first-order differential operators. In holomorphic coordinates and a holomorphic frame of \(E\), the coefficients of these operators at a point are universal expressions in the metric coefficients \(h_{j\bar k}\) of \(X\), the matrix \(H\) of \(h\), and their first derivatives at that point. Indeed \(\bar\partial _E\) acts componentwise, \(D' = \partial + \theta \) where the connection form \(\theta \) involves first derivatives of \(H\), and the formal adjoints are obtained by integration by parts, which differentiates the pointwise inner products and the volume form once. Given \(p\), choose coordinates osculating to order two (Proposition 44.3.2) and a frame as in Lemma 47.3.2. The data then agree to first order at \(p\) with the flat metric on \(\mathbb {C}^n\) and the trivial bundle \(\mathbb {C}^r\) with the standard metric. For these, an \(E\)-valued form is an \(r\)-tuple of scalar forms, \(\bar\partial _E = \bar\partial \) and \(D' = \partial \) act componentwise, and the identities are Theorem 45.3.3. So both sides agree at \(p\), as in Lemma 45.3.1.

01IN Theorem 47.3.4 Bochner–Kodaira–Nakano identity

Let \(X\) be Kähler and \((E, h)\) a Hermitian holomorphic vector bundle with curvature \(F\). Then on \(\mathcal{A}^{p,q}(X, E)\)

\[ \Delta '' = \Delta ' + [iF, \Lambda ], \]

where \(iF\) acts by wedge product combined with the action of \(\operatorname {End}E\).

Proof â–¼

By Theorem 47.3.3, \(\bar\partial _E^* = -i[\Lambda , D']\) and \(D'^* = i[\Lambda , \bar\partial _E]\). Writing \(\bar\partial \) for \(\bar\partial _E\),

\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta ^{\prime \prime } & = -i\left(\bar\partial \Lambda D^{\prime } - \bar\partial D^{\prime }\Lambda + \Lambda D^{\prime }\bar\partial - D^{\prime }\Lambda \bar\partial \right), \\ \Delta ^{\prime } & = i\left(D^{\prime }\Lambda \bar\partial - D^{\prime }\bar\partial \Lambda + \Lambda \bar\partial D^{\prime } - \bar\partial \Lambda D^{\prime }\right). \end{aligned} \]

Subtracting, the terms \(\bar\partial \Lambda D'\) and \(D'\Lambda \bar\partial \) cancel, and

\[ \Delta '' - \Delta ' = i(\bar\partial D' + D'\bar\partial )\Lambda - i\Lambda (D'\bar\partial + \bar\partial D') = iF\Lambda - \Lambda iF = [iF, \Lambda ] . \]
