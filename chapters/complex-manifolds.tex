\chapter{Complex Manifolds}\label{complex-manifolds}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Complex manifolds, their basic examples and the calculus of $(p,q)$-forms.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{smooth-manifolds} (Smooth Manifolds), Chapter~\ref{several-complex-variables} (Several Complex Variables), Chapter~\ref{multilinear-algebra} (Multilinear Algebra) and Chapter~\ref{vector-bundles} (Vector Bundles).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Complex manifolds and holomorphic maps.
\item Examples: projective spaces, complex tori, Grassmannians, hypersurfaces.
\item Almost complex structures and integrability (the Newlander--Nirenberg theorem).
\item Forms of type $(p,q)$ and the operators $\partial$ and $\bar\partial$.
\item Analytic subvarieties.
\item Blow-ups.
\end{itemize}
