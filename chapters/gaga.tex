\chapter{Analytic Spaces and GAGA}\label{gaga}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The comparison between algebraic and analytic geometry over $\CC$.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{divisors} (Divisors and Line Bundles), Chapter~\ref{kodaira} (Vanishing and Embedding Theorems) and Chapter~\ref{complex-manifolds} (Complex Manifolds).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Complex analytic spaces.
\item The analytification functor.
\item Chow's theorem.
\item Serre's GAGA theorems.
\item Algebraic de Rham cohomology and Grothendieck's comparison theorem.
\item Smooth projective varieties as compact K\"ahler manifolds.
\end{itemize}
