\chapter{Rings and Modules}\label{rings}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Rings, ideals and modules, including tensor products and the structure theory of modules over a principal ideal domain.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{groups} (Groups).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Rings, ideals, quotient rings and ring homomorphisms.
\item Integral domains, fields of fractions, prime and maximal ideals.
\item Euclidean domains, principal ideal domains and unique factorisation domains; Gauss's lemma.
\item Polynomial rings and power series rings.
\item Modules, submodules, quotients and exact sequences; free modules, direct sums and products.
\item $\Hom$ and tensor products of modules, and the adjunction between them.
\item Finitely generated modules over a principal ideal domain: the structure theorem.
\item Algebras over a commutative ring; graded rings and modules.
\end{itemize}
