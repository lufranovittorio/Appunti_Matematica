\chapter{Distributions and Sobolev Spaces}\label{distributions}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Distributions, the Fourier transform and Sobolev spaces on Euclidean space and on the torus.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{functional-analysis} (Functional Analysis).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Test functions and distributions; derivatives of distributions.
\item The Fourier transform on Schwartz space and Plancherel's theorem.
\item Sobolev spaces $H^s$ on $\RR^n$ and on the torus.
\item The Sobolev embedding theorem and Rellich's compactness theorem.
\item Elliptic estimates for operators with constant coefficients.
\end{itemize}
