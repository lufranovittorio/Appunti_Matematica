\chapter{Differential Forms and Integration}\label{differential-forms}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Differential forms, the exterior derivative and Stokes' theorem.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{vector-bundles} (Vector Bundles) and Chapter~\ref{measure-theory} (Measure and Integration).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Differential forms, the exterior derivative and pullback.
\item Lie derivative, interior product and Cartan's formula.
\item Orientations of manifolds.
\item Integration of forms on oriented manifolds.
\item Manifolds with boundary and Stokes' theorem.
\end{itemize}
