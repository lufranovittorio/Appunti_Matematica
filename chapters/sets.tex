\chapter{Set Theory}\label{sets}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The axioms of set theory and the basic constructions used everywhere else, including the size conventions needed for category theory.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{logic} (Mathematical Logic).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The axioms of ZFC; pairs, products, relations and functions.
\item Equivalence relations, quotients, partial and total orders.
\item The natural numbers, induction and definitions by recursion.
\item Ordinals, transfinite induction and transfinite recursion.
\item Cardinals and cardinal arithmetic; countable and uncountable sets.
\item The axiom of choice, Zorn's lemma and the well-ordering theorem, with proofs of their equivalence.
\item Classes, Grothendieck universes and the conventions on size used for categories.
\end{itemize}
