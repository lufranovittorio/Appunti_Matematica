\chapter{\'Etale Cohomology}\label{etale-cohomology}
\chapterstatus{planned}

\'Etale and $\ell$-adic cohomology, needed to state the Tate conjecture and to define absolute Hodge classes.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{schemes} (Schemes), Chapter~\ref{sheaf-cohomology} (Sheaf Cohomology) and Chapter~\ref{fields} (Fields and Galois Theory).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \'Etale morphisms and the \'etale site.
\item \'Etale sheaves and their cohomology.
\item $\ell$-adic cohomology.
\item The comparison theorem with singular cohomology.
\item Galois actions on cohomology.
\item The $\ell$-adic cycle class map.
\end{itemize}
