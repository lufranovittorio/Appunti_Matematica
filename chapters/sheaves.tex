\chapter{Sheaves on Topological Spaces}\label{sheaves}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Presheaves and sheaves, the abelian category of sheaves and the operations on sheaves.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{general-topology} (General Topology), Chapter~\ref{categories} (Categories), Chapter~\ref{homological-algebra} (Homological Algebra) and Chapter~\ref{homotopy} (Homotopy and Covering Spaces).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Presheaves, sheaves and stalks; sheafification.
\item The abelian category of sheaves of abelian groups.
\item Direct image, inverse image and their adjunction; extension by zero.
\item Locally constant sheaves and local systems; their relation to representations of $\pi_1$.
\item Ringed spaces and sheaves of modules; locally free sheaves.
\end{itemize}
