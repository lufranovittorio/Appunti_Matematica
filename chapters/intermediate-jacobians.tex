\chapter{Intermediate Jacobians and Normal Functions}\label{intermediate-jacobians}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The Abel--Jacobi map and invariants of homologically trivial cycles.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{vhs} (Variations of Hodge Structure), Chapter~\ref{abelian-varieties} (Abelian Varieties) and Chapter~\ref{cycle-class} (The Cycle Class Map).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Griffiths intermediate Jacobians.
\item The Abel--Jacobi map.
\item Deligne cohomology.
\item Griffiths' theorem on the Griffiths group.
\item Normal functions and Poincar\'e's approach to the Lefschetz $(1,1)$ theorem.
\end{itemize}
