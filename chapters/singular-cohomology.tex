\chapter{Singular Cohomology}\label{singular-cohomology}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Cohomology, its ring structure and the classical computational theorems.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{singular-homology} (Singular Homology).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Singular cochains and cohomology with coefficients.
\item The universal coefficient theorem.
\item Cup product and the cohomology ring.
\item Cap product.
\item The K\"unneth formula.
\item Cohomology rings of projective spaces and tori.
\end{itemize}
