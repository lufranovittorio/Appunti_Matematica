\chapter{Homotopy and Covering Spaces}\label{homotopy}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Homotopy, the fundamental group and covering spaces, together with the CW complexes used throughout algebraic topology.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{general-topology} (General Topology) and Chapter~\ref{groups} (Groups).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Homotopies, homotopy equivalences and contractible spaces.
\item The fundamental group and its functoriality; $\pi_1(S^1) \cong \ZZ$.
\item The Seifert--van Kampen theorem.
\item Covering spaces, lifting properties and the classification of coverings.
\item CW complexes and their basic properties.
\item Higher homotopy groups and the long exact sequence of a fibration.
\end{itemize}
