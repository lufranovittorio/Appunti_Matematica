\chapter{Variations of Hodge Structure}\label{vhs}
\chapterstatus{planned}

How Hodge structures vary in families, and where Hodge classes appear.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{hodge-structures} (Hodge Structures), Chapter~\ref{connections} (Connections and Curvature) and Chapter~\ref{gaga} (Analytic Spaces and GAGA).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Ehresmann's theorem and the Gauss--Manin connection.
\item The period map and Griffiths transversality.
\item Period domains.
\item The theorem of the fixed part.
\item Hodge loci and the theorem of Cattani, Deligne and Kaplan.
\end{itemize}
