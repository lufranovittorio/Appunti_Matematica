\chapter{Algebraic Curves and Riemann Surfaces}\label{curves}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Compact Riemann surfaces: the first testing ground for everything in this project.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{gaga} (Analytic Spaces and GAGA).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Compact Riemann surfaces are projective algebraic curves.
\item The Riemann--Roch theorem.
\item Genus, the canonical divisor and the Riemann--Hurwitz formula.
\item Hyperelliptic curves and the canonical embedding.
\item Abel's theorem and the Jacobi inversion theorem.
\end{itemize}
