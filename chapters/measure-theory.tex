\chapter{Measure and Integration}\label{measure-theory}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Lebesgue integration and the function spaces used in analysis on manifolds.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{real-analysis} (Real Analysis).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item $\sigma$-algebras, measures and outer measures; Carath\'eodory's construction.
\item Lebesgue measure on $\RR^n$; sets of measure zero.
\item The Lebesgue integral and the convergence theorems.
\item $L^p$ spaces and their completeness.
\item Product measures and the Fubini--Tonelli theorem.
\item Change of variables in $\RR^n$.
\item The Radon--Nikodym theorem and the Riesz representation theorem for measures.
\end{itemize}
