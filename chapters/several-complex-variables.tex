\chapter{Several Complex Variables}\label{several-complex-variables}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The local theory of holomorphic functions in several variables, including the $\bar\partial$-Poincar\'e lemma.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{complex-analysis} (Complex Analysis) and Chapter~\ref{commutative-algebra} (Commutative Algebra).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Holomorphic functions on domains in $\CC^n$; the Cauchy formula on polydiscs.
\item Hartogs' extension theorem.
\item The Weierstrass preparation and division theorems.
\item The ring $\mathcal{O}_{\CC^n,0}$ is a Noetherian unique factorisation domain.
\item The $\bar\partial$-Poincar\'e lemma (Dolbeault--Grothendieck lemma).
\item Local structure of analytic sets.
\end{itemize}
