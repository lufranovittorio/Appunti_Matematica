\chapter{Spectral Sequences}\label{spectral-sequences}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Spectral sequences as a computational tool, with complete proofs of the constructions used later.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{homological-algebra} (Homological Algebra).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Spectral sequences, their pages and convergence.
\item The spectral sequence of a filtered complex.
\item The two spectral sequences of a double complex.
\item Exact couples.
\item The Grothendieck spectral sequence of a composition of functors.
\item Degeneration criteria and edge maps.
\end{itemize}
