\chapter{Hard Lefschetz and the Hodge--Riemann Relations}\label{lefschetz-theorems}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The $\mathfrak{sl}_2$-action on the cohomology of a compact K\"ahler manifold and its consequences.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{hodge-decomposition} (The Hodge Decomposition).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The Lefschetz operator and its adjoint on cohomology.
\item The hard Lefschetz theorem.
\item The Lefschetz decomposition and primitive cohomology.
\item The Hodge--Riemann bilinear relations.
\item The Hodge index theorem.
\end{itemize}
