\chapter{Homological Algebra}\label{homological-algebra}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Abelian categories, complexes and derived functors.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{categories} (Categories) and Chapter~\ref{rings} (Rings and Modules).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Additive and abelian categories; exact functors.
\item Chain complexes, homology and the long exact sequence; the snake lemma and the five lemma.
\item Chain homotopies and mapping cones.
\item Projective and injective objects and resolutions; modules have enough injectives.
\item Left and right derived functors; $\operatorname{Ext}$ and $\operatorname{Tor}$.
\item Double complexes and total complexes.
\item Universal coefficient and K\"unneth theorems for complexes of free modules.
\end{itemize}
