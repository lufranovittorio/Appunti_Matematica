\chapter{Singular Homology}\label{singular-homology}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Construction of singular homology and the tools for computing it.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{homotopy} (Homotopy and Covering Spaces) and Chapter~\ref{homological-algebra} (Homological Algebra).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Singular chains and singular homology.
\item Homotopy invariance.
\item Relative homology and the long exact sequence of a pair.
\item Excision (via barycentric subdivision) and the Mayer--Vietoris sequence.
\item Homology of spheres, degree of maps and Brouwer's fixed point theorem.
\item Cellular homology of CW complexes.
\item Homology with coefficients.
\item The Hurewicz map in degree one.
\end{itemize}
