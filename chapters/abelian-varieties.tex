\chapter{Abelian Varieties}\label{abelian-varieties}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Complex tori and abelian varieties: the main source of both evidence and difficulty for the Hodge conjecture.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{curves} (Algebraic Curves and Riemann Surfaces), Chapter~\ref{divisors} (Divisors and Line Bundles), Chapter~\ref{hodge-decomposition} (The Hodge Decomposition) and Chapter~\ref{algebraic-groups} (Linear Algebraic Groups).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Complex tori and their cohomology.
\item Riemann relations and polarisations; the Appell--Humbert theorem.
\item Theta functions and projective embeddings.
\item The dual abelian variety.
\item Jacobians of curves.
\item Endomorphism algebras and complex multiplication.
\end{itemize}
