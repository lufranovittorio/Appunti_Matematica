\chapter{Topological Manifolds and Poincar\'e Duality}\label{poincare-duality}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Orientations and fundamental classes of manifolds, and the duality theorems that turn subvarieties into cohomology classes.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{singular-cohomology} (Singular Cohomology).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Topological manifolds and local homology.
\item Orientations and the orientation double cover.
\item Fundamental classes of closed oriented manifolds.
\item Cohomology with compact supports and Poincar\'e duality.
\item Lefschetz and Alexander duality.
\item The intersection form and the signature.
\item Borel--Moore homology and fundamental classes of closed subsets.
\end{itemize}
