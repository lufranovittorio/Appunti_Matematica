\chapter{Linear Algebra}\label{linear-algebra}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Vector spaces and linear maps, with emphasis on the parts of linear algebra that Hodge theory uses: bilinear and Hermitian forms and complex structures on real vector spaces.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{rings} (Rings and Modules).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Vector spaces, bases and dimension; linear maps and matrices.
\item Duality, the double dual, transposes and annihilators.
\item Determinants and the characteristic polynomial.
\item Eigenvalues, diagonalisation and the Jordan normal form.
\item Bilinear and quadratic forms; Sylvester's law of inertia.
\item Inner product spaces, Hermitian forms, orthogonal and unitary groups, the spectral theorem.
\item Symplectic vector spaces.
\item Complex structures on real vector spaces, complexification and the decomposition $V_\CC = V^{1,0} \oplus V^{0,1}$.
\end{itemize}
