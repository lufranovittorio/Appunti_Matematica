\chapter{The Tate Conjecture}\label{tate-conjecture}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The arithmetic analogue of the Hodge conjecture.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{etale-cohomology} (\'Etale Cohomology), Chapter~\ref{abelian-varieties} (Abelian Varieties) and Chapter~\ref{absolute-hodge} (Absolute Hodge Classes).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Statement of the Tate conjecture.
\item Tate's theorem for abelian varieties over finite fields, and Faltings' theorem.
\item The Mumford--Tate conjecture and its relation to the Hodge conjecture.
\end{itemize}
