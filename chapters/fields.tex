\chapter{Fields and Galois Theory}\label{fields}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Field extensions and Galois theory, including transcendence bases and automorphisms of $\CC$, which are needed for absolute Hodge classes.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{rings} (Rings and Modules) and Chapter~\ref{linear-algebra} (Linear Algebra).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Field extensions, degree and the tower law.
\item Algebraic and transcendental elements; existence and uniqueness of algebraic closures.
\item Splitting fields, normal and separable extensions.
\item The fundamental theorem of Galois theory.
\item Finite fields and cyclotomic extensions.
\item Solvability by radicals.
\item Transcendence bases and transcendence degree; automorphisms of $\CC$.
\item Infinite Galois theory and profinite groups.
\end{itemize}
