\chapter{Schemes}\label{schemes}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The language of schemes, to the extent needed for families, fields of definition and Galois conjugates of varieties.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{varieties} (Algebraic Varieties) and Chapter~\ref{commutative-algebra} (Commutative Algebra).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Spectra of rings and locally ringed spaces.
\item Schemes, morphisms and fibre products.
\item Separated and proper morphisms; valuative criteria.
\item Projective schemes and $\Proj$.
\item Flat and smooth morphisms.
\item Base change and varieties over non-closed fields.
\end{itemize}
