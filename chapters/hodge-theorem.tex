\chapter{Harmonic Forms and the Hodge Theorem}\label{hodge-theorem}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Every de Rham class on a compact oriented Riemannian manifold has a unique harmonic representative.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{elliptic-operators} (Elliptic Operators on Compact Manifolds) and Chapter~\ref{de-rham} (de Rham Cohomology).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Harmonic forms.
\item The Hodge decomposition of the space of forms and the Green operator.
\item The Hodge theorem: de Rham cohomology is isomorphic to the space of harmonic forms.
\item Poincar\'e duality via the Hodge star.
\item Bochner's vanishing theorem.
\end{itemize}
