\chapter{Hodge Structures}\label{hodge-structures}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The linear-algebraic shadow of Hodge theory: pure and polarised Hodge structures.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{lefschetz-theorems} (Hard Lefschetz and the Hodge--Riemann Relations) and Chapter~\ref{algebraic-groups} (Linear Algebraic Groups).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Pure Hodge structures of weight $k$ and the Hodge filtration.
\item Morphisms, tensor products, duals and Tate twists; the abelian category of Hodge structures.
\item Hodge structures as representations of the Deligne torus.
\item Polarisations; polarisable Hodge structures form a semisimple category.
\item Hodge classes.
\item Hodge structures of weight one and complex tori.
\item The Hodge structure on the cohomology of a compact K\"ahler manifold.
\end{itemize}
