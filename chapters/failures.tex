\chapter{Variants That Fail}\label{failures}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Natural strengthenings of the Hodge conjecture that are false, and what each failure teaches.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{known-cases} (Known Cases and Evidence).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The integral Hodge conjecture: the torsion counterexamples of Atiyah and Hirzebruch.
\item Koll\'ar's non-torsion counterexample.
\item Voisin's counterexample for compact K\"ahler manifolds.
\item Hodge's general conjecture and Grothendieck's correction.
\item The variational Hodge conjecture.
\end{itemize}
