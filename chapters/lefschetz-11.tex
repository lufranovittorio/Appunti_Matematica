\chapter{The Lefschetz (1,1) Theorem}\label{lefschetz-11}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The one case of the Hodge conjecture that is completely proved, and everything that follows from it.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{hodge-conjecture} (The Hodge Conjecture) and Chapter~\ref{gaga} (Analytic Spaces and GAGA).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The exponential sequence and the key lemma.
\item Proof of the Lefschetz $(1,1)$ theorem.
\item The Hodge conjecture for divisors and, via hard Lefschetz, for curves.
\item The Hodge conjecture in dimension at most three.
\item Why the proof does not generalise.
\end{itemize}
