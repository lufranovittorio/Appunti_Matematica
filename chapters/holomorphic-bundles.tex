\chapter{Holomorphic Vector Bundles}\label{holomorphic-bundles}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Holomorphic vector bundles and line bundles, divisors and the first Chern class.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{complex-manifolds} (Complex Manifolds), Chapter~\ref{sheaf-cohomology} (Sheaf Cohomology) and Chapter~\ref{characteristic-classes} (Characteristic Classes).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Holomorphic vector bundles and locally free sheaves of $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules.
\item Line bundles and $\Pic(X) \cong H^1(X, \mathcal{O}_X^*)$.
\item Divisors and their line bundles.
\item The exponential sequence and the first Chern class.
\item Hermitian metrics, the Chern connection and its curvature.
\item The canonical bundle and the adjunction formula.
\item Tautological bundles on projective space.
\end{itemize}
