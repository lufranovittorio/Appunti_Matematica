\chapter{Vector Bundles}\label{vector-bundles}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Real and complex vector bundles and the operations on them.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{smooth-manifolds} (Smooth Manifolds), Chapter~\ref{multilinear-algebra} (Multilinear Algebra) and Chapter~\ref{sheaves} (Sheaves on Topological Spaces).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Vector bundles, transition functions and cocycles.
\item Sections and the sheaf of sections.
\item Tangent and cotangent bundles.
\item Direct sums, duals, tensor products, exterior powers and pullbacks.
\item Subbundles, quotients and exact sequences of bundles.
\item Line bundles and \v{C}ech cohomology in degree one.
\end{itemize}
