\chapter{Mumford--Tate Groups}\label{mumford-tate}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The symmetry group of a Hodge structure, which determines its Hodge classes.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{hodge-structures} (Hodge Structures) and Chapter~\ref{abelian-varieties} (Abelian Varieties).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The Mumford--Tate group of a Hodge structure.
\item Hodge classes as invariants of the Mumford--Tate group.
\item Special Mumford--Tate groups and the Hodge group.
\item Mumford--Tate groups of abelian varieties.
\end{itemize}
