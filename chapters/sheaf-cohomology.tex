\chapter{Sheaf Cohomology}\label{sheaf-cohomology}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Sheaf cohomology as a derived functor and its comparison with other cohomology theories.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{sheaves} (Sheaves on Topological Spaces), Chapter~\ref{spectral-sequences} (Spectral Sequences), Chapter~\ref{derived-categories} (Derived Categories) and Chapter~\ref{singular-cohomology} (Singular Cohomology).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Sheaves have enough injectives; cohomology as a derived functor.
\item Flasque, soft and fine sheaves; acyclic resolutions.
\item \v{C}ech cohomology and Leray's theorem.
\item Comparison with singular cohomology on locally contractible spaces.
\item Hypercohomology of complexes of sheaves.
\item The Leray spectral sequence.
\item Cohomology with compact supports.
\end{itemize}
