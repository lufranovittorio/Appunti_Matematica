\chapter{Quasi-coherent and Coherent Sheaves}\label{coherent-sheaves}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Quasi-coherent sheaves, the algebraic counterpart of vector bundles.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{schemes} (Schemes).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Quasi-coherent sheaves on affine and general schemes.
\item Coherent sheaves on Noetherian schemes.
\item Locally free sheaves and vector bundles.
\item The twisting sheaves $\mathcal{O}(n)$ on projective space; Serre's generation theorem.
\item Sheaves of differentials and the canonical sheaf.
\end{itemize}
