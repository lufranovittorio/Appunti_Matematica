\chapter{Connections and Curvature}\label{connections}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Connections on vector bundles, their curvature and parallel transport.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{vector-bundles} (Vector Bundles), Chapter~\ref{differential-forms} (Differential Forms and Integration) and Chapter~\ref{lie-groups} (Lie Groups).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Connections on vector bundles and their curvature.
\item Parallel transport and holonomy.
\item Flat connections and local systems.
\item Metric connections on Euclidean and Hermitian bundles.
\item Principal bundles and their connections.
\end{itemize}
