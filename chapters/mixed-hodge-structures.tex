\chapter{Mixed Hodge Structures}\label{mixed-hodge-structures}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Deligne's mixed Hodge structures on the cohomology of arbitrary complex algebraic varieties.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{hodge-structures} (Hodge Structures), Chapter~\ref{derived-categories} (Derived Categories) and Chapter~\ref{gaga} (Analytic Spaces and GAGA).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Mixed Hodge structures and strictness of morphisms.
\item The logarithmic de Rham complex.
\item The mixed Hodge structure on the cohomology of a smooth variety.
\item Singular varieties via simplicial resolutions (overview).
\item Weights and their applications.
\end{itemize}
