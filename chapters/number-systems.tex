\chapter{Number Systems}\label{number-systems}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Construction of the integers, the rational numbers, the real numbers and the complex numbers, with the properties that analysis and algebra rely on.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{sets} (Set Theory).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The integers and the rational numbers as quotients.
\item Ordered fields and the least upper bound property.
\item Construction of the real numbers (Dedekind cuts and Cauchy sequences) and uniqueness of the complete ordered field.
\item Uncountability of $\RR$.
\item The complex numbers, complex conjugation and the absolute value.
\item Elementary arithmetic in $\ZZ$: Euclid's algorithm, primes, unique factorisation.
\end{itemize}
