\chapter{K\"ahler Manifolds}\label{kahler}
\chapterstatus{planned}

K\"ahler metrics, their local structure and the linear algebra of the Lefschetz operator.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{dolbeault} (Dolbeault Cohomology), Chapter~\ref{riemannian-geometry} (Riemannian Geometry) and Chapter~\ref{representation-theory} (Representations of Groups and Lie Algebras).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Hermitian metrics and the fundamental form.
\item The K\"ahler condition and its equivalent formulations.
\item The Fubini--Study metric; complex submanifolds of K\"ahler manifolds are K\"ahler.
\item Projective manifolds are K\"ahler.
\item Linear algebra of the Lefschetz operator: the $\mathfrak{sl}_2$-action on forms and the primitive decomposition.
\end{itemize}
