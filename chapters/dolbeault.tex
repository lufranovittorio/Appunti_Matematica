\chapter{Dolbeault Cohomology}\label{dolbeault}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Dolbeault cohomology and its identification with the sheaf cohomology of holomorphic forms.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{holomorphic-bundles} (Holomorphic Vector Bundles) and Chapter~\ref{elliptic-operators} (Elliptic Operators on Compact Manifolds).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The Dolbeault complex with values in a holomorphic vector bundle.
\item The Dolbeault theorem $H^{p,q}_{\bar\partial}(X) \cong H^q(X, \Omega^p_X)$.
\item Harmonic theory for $\bar\partial$ on compact Hermitian manifolds.
\item Serre duality.
\item The Fr\"olicher spectral sequence.
\end{itemize}
