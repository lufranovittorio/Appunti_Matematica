\chapter{Lie Groups}\label{lie-groups}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Lie groups, their Lie algebras and homogeneous spaces.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{smooth-manifolds} (Smooth Manifolds) and Chapter~\ref{representation-theory} (Representations of Groups and Lie Algebras).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Lie groups and their Lie algebras.
\item The exponential map.
\item Lie subgroups and the closed subgroup theorem.
\item Homogeneous spaces and quotients.
\item Compact Lie groups; the unitary and orthogonal groups.
\item Haar measure on compact groups.
\end{itemize}
