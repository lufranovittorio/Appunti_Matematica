\chapter{Groups}\label{groups}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Basic group theory, up to the structure results used in topology (presentations, free groups) and in algebra (finitely generated abelian groups, solvability).

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{sets} (Set Theory) and Chapter~\ref{categories} (Categories).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Groups, subgroups, homomorphisms, cosets and Lagrange's theorem.
\item Normal subgroups, quotient groups and the isomorphism theorems.
\item Group actions, orbits and stabilisers, the class equation.
\item The Sylow theorems.
\item Symmetric and alternating groups; simplicity of $A_n$ for $n \geq 5$.
\item Direct and semidirect products; solvable and nilpotent groups.
\item Free groups, presentations, free products and amalgamated products.
\item Abelian groups, torsion, and the structure of finitely generated abelian groups.
\end{itemize}
