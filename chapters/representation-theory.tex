\chapter{Representations of Groups and Lie Algebras}\label{representation-theory}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Representations of finite groups and of Lie algebras, culminating in the representation theory of $\mathfrak{sl}_2$, which organises the Lefschetz theorems.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{groups} (Groups), Chapter~\ref{linear-algebra} (Linear Algebra) and Chapter~\ref{multilinear-algebra} (Multilinear Algebra).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Representations, subrepresentations, irreducibility and Schur's lemma.
\item Maschke's theorem: complete reducibility for finite groups.
\item Characters of finite groups and the orthogonality relations.
\item Lie algebras, ideals and representations; the adjoint representation.
\item Finite-dimensional representations of $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\CC)$: weights, classification and complete reducibility.
\item Overview of semisimple Lie algebras and their representations.
\end{itemize}
