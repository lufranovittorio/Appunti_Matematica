\chapter{Cohomology Operations}\label{cohomology-operations}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Steenrod operations and the representability of cohomology; they give the topological obstructions behind the failure of the integral Hodge conjecture.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{poincare-duality} (Topological Manifolds and Poincar\'e Duality) and Chapter~\ref{homotopy} (Homotopy and Covering Spaces).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Eilenberg--MacLane spaces.
\item Representability of cohomology by Eilenberg--MacLane spaces.
\item Steenrod squares and reduced power operations; the Adem relations.
\item Cohomology of $K(\ZZ/p, n)$ in low degrees.
\item Overview of complex cobordism.
\end{itemize}
