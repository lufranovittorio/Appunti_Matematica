\chapter{Elliptic Operators on Compact Manifolds}\label{elliptic-operators}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The analytic core of Hodge theory: regularity and Fredholm theory for elliptic operators on compact manifolds.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{riemannian-geometry} (Riemannian Geometry) and Chapter~\ref{distributions} (Distributions and Sobolev Spaces).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Differential operators on vector bundles and their principal symbols.
\item Sobolev spaces of sections on compact manifolds.
\item Elliptic estimates and elliptic regularity.
\item Elliptic operators are Fredholm.
\item Spectral decomposition of self-adjoint elliptic operators.
\end{itemize}
