\chapter{Absolute Hodge Classes}\label{absolute-hodge}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Hodge classes that are preserved by the automorphisms of $\CC$.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{hodge-conjecture} (The Hodge Conjecture), Chapter~\ref{etale-cohomology} (\'Etale Cohomology), Chapter~\ref{gaga} (Analytic Spaces and GAGA) and Chapter~\ref{mumford-tate} (Mumford--Tate Groups).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Conjugate varieties and comparison isomorphisms.
\item Absolute Hodge classes.
\item Deligne's theorem: Hodge classes on abelian varieties are absolute.
\item Fields of definition of Hodge loci.
\end{itemize}
