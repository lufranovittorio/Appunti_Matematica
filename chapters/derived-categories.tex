\chapter{Derived Categories}\label{derived-categories}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Triangulated and derived categories, including the filtered derived categories that underlie mixed Hodge theory.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{homological-algebra} (Homological Algebra) and Chapter~\ref{spectral-sequences} (Spectral Sequences).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The homotopy category of complexes and its triangulated structure.
\item Localisation of categories; the derived category of an abelian category.
\item Derived functors between derived categories and their composition.
\item Hypercohomology.
\item $t$-structures (overview).
\item Filtered derived categories and strict morphisms.
\end{itemize}
