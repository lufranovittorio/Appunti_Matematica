\chapter{The Hodge Decomposition}\label{hodge-decomposition}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The K\"ahler identities and the Hodge decomposition of the cohomology of a compact K\"ahler manifold.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{kahler} (K\"ahler Manifolds) and Chapter~\ref{hodge-theorem} (Harmonic Forms and the Hodge Theorem).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The K\"ahler identities.
\item Comparison of Laplacians: $\Delta_d = 2\Delta_{\bar\partial} = 2\Delta_\partial$.
\item The Hodge decomposition $H^k(X,\CC) = \bigoplus_{p+q=k} H^{p,q}(X)$ and Hodge symmetry.
\item The $\partial\bar\partial$-lemma.
\item Hodge numbers of curves, projective spaces, tori and hypersurfaces.
\item A non-K\"ahler counterexample: the Hopf surface.
\end{itemize}
