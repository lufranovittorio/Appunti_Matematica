\chapter{Commutative Algebra}\label{commutative-algebra}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The algebraic foundations of algebraic geometry and of the local theory of analytic functions.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{rings} (Rings and Modules) and Chapter~\ref{fields} (Fields and Galois Theory).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Prime spectrum, nilradical and Jacobson radical.
\item Localisation of rings and modules.
\item Noetherian and Artinian rings; Hilbert's basis theorem.
\item Integral extensions; going up and going down.
\item Hilbert's Nullstellensatz.
\item Primary decomposition and associated primes.
\item Dimension theory: Krull dimension, Krull's principal ideal theorem, systems of parameters.
\item Regular local rings, discrete valuation rings and Dedekind domains.
\item Completions, the Artin--Rees lemma and Krull's intersection theorem.
\item Flatness and K\"ahler differentials.
\end{itemize}
