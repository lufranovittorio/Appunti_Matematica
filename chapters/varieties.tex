\chapter{Algebraic Varieties}\label{varieties}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Classical algebraic varieties over an algebraically closed field.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{commutative-algebra} (Commutative Algebra), Chapter~\ref{general-topology} (General Topology) and Chapter~\ref{sheaves} (Sheaves on Topological Spaces).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Affine varieties, the Zariski topology and the Nullstellensatz dictionary.
\item Projective varieties and homogeneous coordinates.
\item Regular functions and morphisms; varieties as ringed spaces.
\item Dimension.
\item Tangent spaces and smoothness.
\item Rational maps and birational equivalence.
\item Blow-ups.
\end{itemize}
