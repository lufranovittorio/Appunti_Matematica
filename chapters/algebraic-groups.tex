\chapter{Linear Algebraic Groups}\label{algebraic-groups}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Linear algebraic groups and their representations, as needed for Mumford--Tate groups.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{schemes} (Schemes), Chapter~\ref{lie-groups} (Lie Groups) and Chapter~\ref{representation-theory} (Representations of Groups and Lie Algebras).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Affine group schemes and linear algebraic groups.
\item Representations and the Tannakian point of view.
\item Tori and characters; the Deligne torus.
\item Reductive groups (overview).
\item The Lie algebra of an algebraic group.
\item Weil restriction of scalars.
\end{itemize}
