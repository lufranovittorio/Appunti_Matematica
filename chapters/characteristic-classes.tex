\chapter{Characteristic Classes}\label{characteristic-classes}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Chern classes of complex vector bundles, from both the differential-geometric and the topological point of view.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{connections} (Connections and Curvature), Chapter~\ref{de-rham} (de Rham Cohomology) and Chapter~\ref{cohomology-operations} (Cohomology Operations).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Chern--Weil theory: characteristic forms from curvature.
\item Chern classes of complex vector bundles and their axiomatic characterisation.
\item The splitting principle; the Chern character and the Todd class.
\item The Thom isomorphism and the Euler class.
\item Pontryagin classes.
\item Classifying spaces and Grassmannians.
\end{itemize}
