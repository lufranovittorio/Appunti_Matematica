\chapter{Riemannian Geometry}\label{riemannian-geometry}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Riemannian metrics and the operators (Hodge star, codifferential, Laplacian) that lead to harmonic forms.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{connections} (Connections and Curvature).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Riemannian metrics and the Levi-Civita connection.
\item Geodesics, the exponential map and the Hopf--Rinow theorem.
\item The curvature tensor; Ricci and scalar curvature.
\item The volume form, the Hodge star operator and the codifferential.
\item The Hodge Laplacian and the Weitzenb\"ock formula.
\end{itemize}
