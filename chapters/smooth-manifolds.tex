\chapter{Smooth Manifolds}\label{smooth-manifolds}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Smooth manifolds, tangent spaces, vector fields and flows.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{real-analysis} (Real Analysis), Chapter~\ref{general-topology} (General Topology) and Chapter~\ref{measure-theory} (Measure and Integration).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Smooth manifolds and smooth maps.
\item Tangent spaces and the differential.
\item Immersions, submersions and submanifolds; the regular value theorem.
\item Sard's theorem.
\item Partitions of unity.
\item Vector fields, integral curves and flows; the Lie bracket.
\item The Whitney embedding theorem.
\end{itemize}
