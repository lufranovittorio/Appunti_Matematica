60.5 Cycles and coherent sheaves01TX

Complete. This chapter is written; its proofs await independent checking.

01TY Proposition 60.5.1

Let \(X\) be a nonsingular projective variety and \(Z \subseteq X\) a subvariety of codimension \(p\). Then \(\mathrm{ch}(\mathcal{O}_Z) = [Z] + (\text{terms in } \operatorname {CH}^{{\gt}p}(X) \otimes \mathbb {Q})\), where \(\mathrm{ch}\) is computed with a finite locally free resolution of \(\mathcal{O}_Z\). Consequently the \(\mathbb {Q}\)-subspace of \(H^{2\bullet }(X; \mathbb {Q})\) spanned by the classes of algebraic cycles equals the \(\mathbb {Q}\)-subalgebra generated by the Chern classes of algebraic vector bundles on \(X\).

Proof â–¼

The first statement is in [Ful98, Chapter 15]: it follows from Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch (Theorem 59.7.2) applied to a resolution of singularities of \(Z\), or directly from the fact that the lowest nonzero term of \(\mathrm{ch}\) of a sheaf supported in codimension \(p\) is its fundamental cycle. On a nonsingular projective variety every coherent sheaf has a finite resolution by locally free sheaves [Har77, Exercises III.6.8 and III.6.9], so \(\mathrm{ch}(\mathcal{O}_Z)\) is a polynomial with rational coefficients in Chern classes of vector bundles. By descending induction on \(p\), every \([Z]\) lies in the \(\mathbb {Q}\)-algebra generated by Chern classes of vector bundles. Conversely, Chern classes of vector bundles are classes of algebraic cycles, \(c_i(E) \cap [X] \in \operatorname {CH}^i(X)\) (Theorem 60.3.1(3)), and products of cycle classes are cycle classes (Theorem 60.3.1(1)).

01TZ Remark 60.5.2

Proposition 60.5.1 suggests versions of the Hodge conjecture for compact Kähler manifolds: with analytic cycles, with Chern classes of holomorphic vector bundles, or with Chern classes of coherent analytic sheaves. All of these fail for suitable complex tori: Voisin constructed a four-dimensional torus with a nonzero Hodge class in \(H^4\) that is not a combination of Chern classes of coherent sheaves [Voi02a]. So projectivity is essential (Chapter 70).

01U0 Remark 60.5.3

The cycle class map refines to the Deligne cycle class with values in Deligne cohomology \(H^{2p}_{\mathcal{D}}(X; \mathbb {Z}(p))\). Its restriction to homologically trivial cycles is the Abel–Jacobi map to the intermediate Jacobian (Chapter 68), which sees information about \(\operatorname {CH}^p(X)\) invisible to cohomology, such as \(\operatorname {Pic}^0\) for \(p = 1\).
