Complete. This chapter is written; its proofs await independent checking.

00GF Exercise 7.12.1

Let \(k\) be a field. Show that \(M_n(k)\) has no two-sided ideals other than \(0\) and \(M_n(k)\), although it has many left ideals for \(n \geq 2\).

00GG Exercise 7.12.2

Show that \(\mathbb {Z}[x]/(x^2 + 1) \cong \mathbb {Z}[i]\), and that \(\mathbb {R}[x]/(x^2 + 1) \cong \mathbb {C}\).

00GH Exercise 7.12.3

Show that \((\mathbb {Z}/n\mathbb {Z})^\times \) consists of the classes of integers coprime to \(n\), and deduce Fermat’s little theorem: \(a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod p\) for \(p\) prime and \(p \nmid a\).

Solution â–¼

If \(\gcd (a, n) = 1\) then \(ax + ny = 1\) by Theorem 4.5.3, so \([a][x] = 1\). If \(d = \gcd (a, n) {\gt} 1\) then \([a][n/d] = 0\) with \([n/d] \neq 0\), so \([a]\) is a zero divisor and not a unit. For Fermat, apply Theorem 6.3.3 to the group \(\mathbb {F}_p^\times \) of order \(p - 1\).

00GI Exercise 7.12.4

Let \(p\) be prime. Show that \(x^{p-1} + x^{p-2} + \cdots + 1\) is irreducible in \(\mathbb {Q}[x]\) by applying Proposition 7.6.12 to its image under \(x \mapsto x + 1\).

00GJ Exercise 7.12.5

Compute \(\mathbb {Q}\otimes _\mathbb {Z}\mathbb {Q}\) and show that \(\mathbb {Q}\) is a flat \(\mathbb {Z}\)-module which is not free.

00GK Exercise 7.12.6

Let \(k\) be a field. Show that \(k[x, y]\) is a UFD but not a PID.

00GL Exercise 7.12.7

Let \(k\) be a field, \(V\) a finite-dimensional vector space and \(T \colon V \to V\) linear. Using Example 7.7.2 and Theorem 7.11.3, show that \(V \cong \bigoplus _i k[x]/(f_i)\) as \(k[x]\)-modules for monic \(f_1 \mid \cdots \mid f_k\), and that \(f_k\) is the monic polynomial of least degree with \(f_k(T) = 0\).
