52.5 Twisting sheaves on projective schemes01MM

Complete. This chapter is written; its proofs await independent checking.

01MN Definition 52.5.1

Let \(S\) be a graded ring and \(M\) a graded \(S\)-module. The sheaf \(\widetilde M\) on \(\operatorname {Proj}S\) has sections over \(D_+(f)\) equal to \(M_{(f)}\), the degree-zero part of \(M_f\), and stalks \(M_{(\mathfrak {p})}\). For \(n \in \mathbb {Z}\), \(M(n)\) is \(M\) with degrees shifted, \(M(n)_d = M_{n+d}\), and \(\mathcal{O}(n) = S(n)^\sim \). For an \(\mathcal{O}\)-module \(\mathcal{F}\) put \(\mathcal{F}(n) = \mathcal{F} \otimes \mathcal{O}(n)\) and \(\Gamma _*(\mathcal{F}) = \bigoplus _{n \in \mathbb {Z}}\Gamma (X, \mathcal{F}(n))\). For a scheme \(X\) with a closed immersion into \(\mathbb {P}^r_A\), \(\mathcal{O}_X(1)\) is the restriction of \(\mathcal{O}(1)\).

01MO Proposition 52.5.2

Let \(S\) be generated by \(S_1\) as an \(S_0\)-algebra and \(X = \operatorname {Proj}S\). Then \(\mathcal{O}(n)\) is invertible, \(\mathcal{O}(n) \otimes \mathcal{O}(m) \cong \mathcal{O}(n+m)\), and \(\widetilde M(n) \cong (M(n))^\sim \). For \(S = A[x_0, \ldots , x_r]\) and \(X = \mathbb {P}^r_A\),

\[ \Gamma (X, \mathcal{O}(n)) \cong S_n \quad \text{for all } n \in \mathbb {Z}, \]

the homogeneous polynomials of degree \(n\) (zero for \(n {\lt} 0\)); so \(\Gamma _*(\mathcal{O}_X) = S\).

Proof â–¼

Since \(S\) is generated by \(S_1\), the \(D_+(f)\) with \(f \in S_1\) cover \(X\), and on \(D_+(f)\) the module \(S(n)_{(f)}\) is free over \(S_{(f)}\) with basis \(f^n\). This proves invertibility, and the other isomorphisms follow similarly from \(M(n)_{(f)} = M_{(f)} \cdot f^n\). For \(\mathbb {P}^r_A\), a global section of \(\mathcal{O}(n)\) is a compatible family of degree-\(n\) elements \(t_i \in S_{x_i}\). The \(x_i\) are non-zero-divisors, so all \(S_{x_i}\) embed in \(S' = S_{x_0 \cdots x_r}\) and compatibility means that the \(t_i\) are one element \(t\) of \(\bigcap _iS_{x_i}\), whose homogeneous components lie in the intersection. An element of \(S'\) is a sum of Laurent monomials \(x^a\), \(a \in \mathbb {Z}^{r+1}\), with coefficients in \(A\). It lies in \(S_{x_i}\) exactly when only monomials with \(a_j \geq 0\) for \(j \neq i\) occur. Hence \(\bigcap _iS_{x_i} = S\), and the degree-\(n\) part is \(S_n\).

01MP Theorem 52.5.3 Serre

Let \(X\) be a closed subscheme of \(\mathbb {P}^r_A\) with \(A\) Noetherian, and \(\mathcal{F}\) a coherent sheaf on \(X\). Then there is \(n_0\) such that for all \(n \geq n_0\) the sheaf \(\mathcal{F}(n)\) is generated by finitely many global sections. Consequently \(\mathcal{F}\) is a quotient of a finite direct sum of sheaves \(\mathcal{O}_X(-n)\).

Proof â–¼

Let \(i \colon X \to \mathbb {P}^r_A\) be the inclusion. Then \(i_*\mathcal{F}\) is coherent (Proposition 52.3.5, with finiteness checked on affines), and \(i_*(\mathcal{F}(n)) = (i_*\mathcal{F})(n)\) since \(\mathcal{O}_X(1) = i^*\mathcal{O}(1)\). So we may assume \(X = \mathbb {P}^r_A\). Cover \(X\) by \(U_i = D_+(x_i) = X_{x_i}\), where \(x_i \in \Gamma (X, \mathcal{O}(1))\), and write \(\mathcal{F}|_{U_i} = \widetilde{M_i}\) with \(M_i\) generated by finitely many sections \(s_{ij}\). By Lemma 52.3.2(2) with \(\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{O}(1)\) and \(f = x_i\), for \(n\) large each \(s_{ij} \otimes x_i^n\) extends to a global section of \(\mathcal{F}(n)\), and we can take one \(n\) for the finitely many sections. On \(U_i\), \(x_i^n\) generates \(\mathcal{O}(n)\), so the sections \(s_{ij} \otimes x_i^n\) generate \(\mathcal{F}(n)|_{U_i}\). The same holds for all larger \(n\) (multiply by \(x_i\)). Finally, \(N\) global sections generating \(\mathcal{F}(n)\) give a surjection \(\mathcal{O}^N \to \mathcal{F}(n)\), that is, \(\mathcal{O}(-n)^N \to \mathcal{F}\).

01MQ Definition 52.5.4

An invertible sheaf \(\mathcal{L}\) on a scheme \(X\) of finite type over \(A\) is very ample (relative to \(A\)) if \(\mathcal{L} \cong i^*\mathcal{O}(1)\) for an immersion \(i \colon X \to \mathbb {P}^r_A\), and ample if for every coherent \(\mathcal{F}\) the sheaf \(\mathcal{F} \otimes \mathcal{L}^n\) is globally generated for \(n \gg 0\). By Theorem 52.5.3, very ample sheaves on projective schemes over Noetherian rings are ample. The converse relation, that some power of an ample sheaf is very ample, is in Chapter 54.
