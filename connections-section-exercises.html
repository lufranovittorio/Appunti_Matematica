36.6 Exercises01BF

Complete. This chapter is written; its proofs await independent checking.

01BG Exercise 36.6.1

Compute the connection and curvature forms of the Levi-Civita connection of the round sphere \(S^2\) in a local orthonormal frame, and verify that the curvature is \(F = \mathrm{vol}\) times a skew-symmetric matrix, of total integral \(4\pi \) (compare the Gauss–Bonnet theorem, Chapter 37).

01BH Exercise 36.6.2

Give an example of a flat bundle that is not trivial, and of a bundle admitting no flat connection.

Solution â–¼

The Möbius bundle over \(S^1\) is flat (its transition function is locally constant) but not trivial (Exercise 33.5.1). The tautological bundle \(\mathcal{O}(-1)\) on \(\mathbb {C}\mathbb {P}^1\) admits no flat connection: by Theorem 36.3.5 a flat bundle on a simply connected manifold is determined by a representation of the trivial group, hence is trivial, while \(\mathcal{O}(-1)\) is not trivial because \(c_1(\mathcal{O}(-1)) \neq 0\) (Example 33.4.3).

01BI Exercise 36.6.3

Show that for a line bundle with connection, the holonomy is given by \(\exp (-\int _\Sigma F)\) for a loop bounding a surface \(\Sigma \), and deduce that flat line bundles on a simply connected manifold are trivial.

01BJ Exercise 36.6.4

Verify that a connection on \(E\) induces connections on \(E^\vee \) and \(E \otimes F\), and that the curvature of \(E \otimes F\) is \(F_E \otimes 1 + 1 \otimes F_F\).
