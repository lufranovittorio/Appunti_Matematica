01ZV Exercise 69.7.1

Show that every torsion class in \(H^{2p}(X; \mathbb {Z})\) is an integral Hodge class, and that \(Z^{2p}(X)\) is torsion if \(\mathrm{HC}(X, p)\) holds.

Solution â–¼

A torsion class maps to \(0 \in H^{2p}(X; \mathbb {C})\), which lies in \(H^{p,p}\). The second statement is Proposition 69.2.2.

01ZW Exercise 69.7.2

Why does Theorem 69.2.5 not contradict the fact that a quintic threefold contains lines, which have degree \(1\)?

Solution â–¼

The theorem needs \(d\) divisible by \(\ell ^3 \geq 125\), and the hypersurface very general. A quintic has \(d = 5\), which is not divisible by \(125\).
