61.5 Exercises0297

Complete. This chapter is written; its proofs await independent checking.

0298 Exercise 61.5.1

Using Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch on \(\mathbb {P}^1 \times \mathbb {P}^1\), show that \(\chi (\mathcal{O}(p, q)) = (p + 1)(q + 1)\) for the line bundle of bidegree \((p, q)\), and check it for \(p, q \geq 0\) by counting bihomogeneous polynomials.

Solution â–¼

With \(l = pa + qb\) and \(c_1 = 2a + 2b\), Corollary 61.4.6 gives \(\chi = 1 + \frac12\int (pa + qb)((p + 2)a + (q + 2)b) = 1 + \frac12(p(q + 2) + q(p + 2)) = pq + p + q + 1\). For \(p, q \geq 0\) the higher cohomology vanishes (by the Künneth formula and Theorem 53.3.1) and the sections are the polynomials of bidegree \((p, q)\), of which there are \((p + 1)(q + 1)\).

0299 Exercise 61.5.2

Show that the Todd genus of \(\mathbb {P}^n\) is \(1\), and that the Todd genus of a compact complex manifold equals \(\chi (X, \mathcal{O}_X) = \sum _q(-1)^qh^{0,q}(X)\).

Solution â–¼

The second statement is Theorem 61.3.2(3) with \(E = \mathcal{O}_X\), since \(\mathrm{ch}(\mathcal{O}_X) = 1\). For \(\mathbb {P}^n\), Proposition 61.4.5 with \(k = 0\) gives \(\binom {n}{n} = 1\).

029A Exercise 61.5.3

Let \(S \subseteq \mathbb {P}^3\) be a smooth surface of degree \(d\). Using the adjunction formula \(c_1(T_S) = (4 - d)h\) and Noether’s formula, show that \(\int _Sc_2(T_S) = d^3 - 4d^2 + 6d\), and check that this gives \(24\) for a quartic.

Solution â–¼

From the exact sequence \(0 \to \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb {P}^3}(-d) \to \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb {P}^3} \to \mathcal{O}_S \to 0\), additivity of \(\chi \) and Proposition 61.4.5, \(\chi (\mathcal{O}_S) = 1 - \frac{(1 - d)(2 - d)(3 - d)}{6} = 1 + \frac{(d - 1)(d - 2)(d - 3)}{6}\). As \(\int _Sh^2 = d\), \(\int c_1^2 = (4 - d)^2d\). Noether’s formula gives \(\int c_2 = 12\chi (\mathcal{O}_S) - (4 - d)^2d = 12 + 2(d - 1)(d - 2)(d - 3) - d(4 - d)^2 = d^3 - 4d^2 + 6d\). For \(d = 4\): \(64 - 64 + 24 = 24\).
