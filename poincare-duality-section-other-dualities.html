21.7 Other dualities00Y3

Complete. This chapter is written; its proofs await independent checking.

00Y4 Theorem 21.7.1 Lefschetz duality

Let \(M\) be a compact \(R\)-orientable \(n\)-manifold with boundary. There is a fundamental class \([M] \in H_n(M, \partial M; R)\) restricting to local orientations, and cap product with it gives isomorphisms \(H^k(M, \partial M; R) \cong H_{n-k}(M; R)\) and \(H^k(M; R) \cong H_{n-k}(M, \partial M; R)\).

This is [Hat02, Theorem 3.43]; the proof uses a collar neighbourhood of \(\partial M\) to reduce to Theorem 21.6.2 for the interior of \(M\).

00Y5 Theorem 21.7.2 Alexander duality

Let \(K \subsetneq S^n\) be a compact, locally contractible, nonempty subspace. Then \(\tilde{H}_i(S^n \setminus K; \mathbb {Z}) \cong \tilde{H}^{n-i-1}(K; \mathbb {Z})\) for all \(i\).

This is [Hat02, Theorem 3.44]. The idea: \(\tilde{H}_i(S^n \setminus K) \cong H^{n-i}_c(S^n \setminus K)\) by duality, and \(H^{n-i}_c(S^n \setminus K)\) is the colimit of \(H^{n-i}(S^n, U)\) over open neighbourhoods \(U\) of \(K\), which for locally contractible \(K\) is \(H^{n-i}(S^n, K) \cong \tilde{H}^{n-i-1}(K)\).

00Y6 Corollary 21.7.3 Jordan–Brouwer separation theorem

If \(K \subseteq S^n\) is homeomorphic to \(S^{n-1}\), then \(S^n \setminus K\) has exactly two path components. If \(K\) is homeomorphic to \(D^k\), then \(S^n \setminus K\) is path-connected.

Proof â–¼

By Theorem 21.7.2, \(\tilde{H}_0(S^n \setminus K) \cong \tilde{H}^{n-1}(S^{n-1}) \cong \mathbb {Z}\), respectively \(\tilde{H}^{n-1}(D^k) = 0\). Since \(S^n \setminus K\) is an open subset of a manifold, its path components are its components, and \(\tilde{H}_0\) is free of rank one less than their number (Proposition 19.2.4).
