52.7 Exercises01MW

Complete. This chapter is written; its proofs await independent checking.

01MX Exercise 52.7.1

Let \(X = \operatorname {Spec}R\) for a discrete valuation ring \(R\) with fraction field \(K\), and let \(\mathcal{F}\) be the sheaf with \(\mathcal{F}(X) = 0\) and \(\mathcal{F}(\{ \eta \} ) = K\) on the open generic point. Show that \(\mathcal{F}\) is an \(\mathcal{O}_X\)-module that is not quasi-coherent, while \(j_*K\) for \(j \colon \{ \eta \} \to X\) is quasi-coherent.

Solution â–¼

If \(\mathcal{F}\) were quasi-coherent, it would be \(\widetilde{\Gamma (X, \mathcal{F})} = 0\) (Theorem 52.3.3), but \(\mathcal{F}_\eta = K \neq 0\). On the other hand \(j_*K = \widetilde K\), since \(\widetilde K(\{ \eta \} ) = K_{(0)} = K\) and \(\widetilde K(X) = K\).

01MY Exercise 52.7.2

Show that \(\mathcal{O}(-1)\) on \(\mathbb {P}^r_k\) is the sheaf of sections of the tautological line bundle \(\{ (x, \ell ) \in \mathbb {A}^{r+1} \times \mathbb {P}^r : x \in \ell \} \), and that \(\mathcal{O}(1)\) has no nonzero section vanishing on a hyperplane twice.

Solution â–¼

On \(U_i\) the tautological bundle is trivialised by the section \(\ell \mapsto (x_0/x_i, \ldots , x_r/x_i)\), and on \(U_i \cap U_j\) these trivialisations differ by \(x_i/x_j\). This is the cocycle of \(\mathcal{O}(-1)\), whose generator on \(U_i\) is \(1/x_i\): \(1/x_j = (x_i/x_j)(1/x_i)\). A section of \(\mathcal{O}(1)\) is a linear form by Proposition 52.5.2, and a nonzero linear form vanishes on its hyperplane to order exactly \(1\).

01MZ Exercise 52.7.3

Using Proposition 52.6.4, show that a nonsingular plane cubic has trivial canonical sheaf and that a nonsingular plane quartic has \(\omega \cong \mathcal{O}(1)\), so that \(\Gamma (Y, \omega _Y)\) contains the three linear forms. (In fact it is spanned by them, and the genus is \(3\); see Chapter 56.)

Solution â–¼

\(d - n - 1 = 3 - 3 = 0\) and \(4 - 3 = 1\). The restriction \(\Gamma (\mathbb {P}^2, \mathcal{O}(1)) \to \Gamma (Y, \mathcal{O}_Y(1))\) is injective because a linear form vanishing on \(Y\) would be divisible by the quartic equation. So \(\Gamma (Y, \omega _Y)\) contains a \(3\)-dimensional space.

01N0 Exercise 52.7.4

Compute \(\Omega _{X/k}\) for the cusp \(X = \operatorname {Spec}k[x, y]/(y^2 - x^3)\) and show that it is not locally free.

Solution â–¼

By Example 13.12.3, \(\Omega = (B\, dx \oplus B\, dy)/(2y\, dy - 3x^2dx)\). Its fibre at the origin has dimension \(2\), while at other points it has dimension \(1\) (where \(x \neq 0\) or \(y \neq 0\), the relation is nonzero modulo the maximal ideal, in characteristic \(\neq 2, 3\)). As \(X\) is reduced, Proposition 52.3.8(3) shows that \(\Omega \) is not locally free.
