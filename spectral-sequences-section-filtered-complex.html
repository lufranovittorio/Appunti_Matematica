14.3 The spectral sequence of a filtered complex00P9

Complete. This chapter is written; its proofs await independent checking.

00PA Definition 14.3.1

A filtered complex is a complex \(K^\bullet \) with subcomplexes \(\cdots \supseteq F^pK \supseteq F^{p+1}K \supseteq \cdots \). The filtration is finite in each degree if for every \(n\) there are \(a \leq b\) with \(F^aK^n = K^n\) and \(F^bK^n = 0\). The associated graded complexes are \(\mathrm{gr}^pK = F^pK/F^{p+1}K\), and the induced filtration on cohomology is \(F^pH^n(K) = \operatorname {im}(H^n(F^pK) \to H^n(K))\).

00PB Definition 14.3.2

For a filtered complex \(K\) we abbreviate \(F^p = F^pK\) and, for \(r \geq 0\),

\[ Z_r^p = F^p \cap d^{-1}(F^{p+r}), \qquad B_r^p = F^p \cap d(F^{p-r+1}), \qquad E_r^p = Z_r^p/(B_r^p + Z_{r-1}^{p+1}), \]

with the convention \(Z_{-1}^{p+1} = F^{p+1}\). These are graded by the degree of \(K\), and we write \(E_r^{p,q}\) for the part of \(E_r^p\) in degree \(p + q\). Let \(d_r \colon E_r^p \to E_r^{p+r}\) be induced by \(d\).

00PC Lemma 14.3.3

The inclusions \(B_r^p \subseteq Z_r^p\) and \(Z_{r-1}^{p+1} \subseteq Z_r^p\) hold, so \(E_r^p\) is defined, and \(d_r\) is a well-defined morphism with \(d_r^2 = 0\), of bidegree \((r, 1 - r)\). Moreover \(E_0^p = \mathrm{gr}^pK\) with \(d_0\) induced by \(d\), and \(E_1^{p,q} = H^{p+q}(\mathrm{gr}^pK)\).

Proof â–¼

Elements of \(B_r^p\) are boundaries, hence cycles; and \(x \in F^{p+1}\) with \(dx \in F^{p+r}\) lies in \(Z_r^p\). If \(x \in Z_r^p\) then \(dx \in F^{p+r}\) and \(d(dx) = 0\), so \(dx \in Z_r^{p+r}\). We must check that \(d\) maps \(B_r^p + Z_{r-1}^{p+1}\) into \(B_r^{p+r} + Z_{r-1}^{p+r+1}\): \(d(B_r^p) = 0\), and for \(x \in Z_{r-1}^{p+1}\) we have \(dx \in F^{p+r} \cap d(F^{p+1}) = B_r^{p+r}\). Clearly \(d_r^2 = 0\). For \(r = 0\): \(Z_0^p = F^p\) and \(B_0^p = d(F^{p+1}) \subseteq F^{p+1}\), so \(E_0^p = F^p/F^{p+1}\). For \(r = 1\): \(Z_1^p\) consists of the elements of \(F^p\) whose image in \(\mathrm{gr}^p\) is a cocycle, \(Z_0^{p+1} = F^{p+1}\) and \(B_1^p = d(F^p)\), so \(E_1^p = Z_1^p/(d(F^p) + F^{p+1})\) is the cohomology of \(\mathrm{gr}^pK\).

00PD Theorem 14.3.4

For every filtered complex, the objects \(E_r^{p,q}\) and differentials \(d_r\), \(r \geq 0\), form a spectral sequence: \(H(E_r, d_r) \cong E_{r+1}\). If the filtration is finite in each degree, then for fixed \((p, q)\) and all sufficiently large \(r\), \(E_r^{p,q} = E_\infty ^{p,q}\) with

\[ E_\infty ^{p,q} \cong F^pH^{p+q}(K)/F^{p+1}H^{p+q}(K). \]

In particular, if \(F^0K = K\) and \(F^{n+1}K^n = 0\) for all \(n\), the spectral sequence is first-quadrant (from \(E_1\) on, since \(E_1^{p,q}\) vanishes for \(p {\lt} 0\) or \(q {\lt} 0\)) and \(E_1^{p,q} = H^{p+q}(\mathrm{gr}^pK) \Rightarrow H^{p+q}(K)\).

Proof â–¼

Kernel of \(d_r\) at \(E_r^p\). Let \(x \in Z_r^p\) with \(dx \in B_r^{p+r} + Z_{r-1}^{p+r+1}\), say \(dx = dy + z\) with \(y \in F^{p+1}\), \(dy \in F^{p+r}\) and \(z \in F^{p+r+1}\). Then \(y \in Z_{r-1}^{p+1}\), and \(x - y \in F^p\) with \(d(x - y) = z \in F^{p+r+1}\), so \(x - y \in Z_{r+1}^p\). Conversely, \(Z_{r+1}^p\) maps into the kernel, since \(dx \in F^{p+r+1}\) and \(d(dx) = 0\) give \(dx \in Z_{r-1}^{p+r+1}\). Hence the kernel is \((Z_{r+1}^p + Z_{r-1}^{p+1})/(B_r^p + Z_{r-1}^{p+1})\).

Image of \(d_r\) in \(E_r^p\). It consists of the classes of \(dx\) with \(x \in Z_r^{p-r}\), that is of the elements of \(F^p \cap d(F^{p-r}) = B_{r+1}^p\). Since \(B_r^p \subseteq B_{r+1}^p\), the image is \((B_{r+1}^p + Z_{r-1}^{p+1})/(B_r^p + Z_{r-1}^{p+1})\).

Cohomology. Therefore \(H(E_r)^p = (Z_{r+1}^p + Z_{r-1}^{p+1})/(B_{r+1}^p + Z_{r-1}^{p+1})\). The map \(Z_{r+1}^p \to H(E_r)^p\) is surjective, with kernel \(Z_{r+1}^p \cap (B_{r+1}^p + Z_{r-1}^{p+1}) = B_{r+1}^p + (Z_{r+1}^p \cap Z_{r-1}^{p+1})\) (as \(B_{r+1}^p \subseteq Z_{r+1}^p\)). Now \(Z_{r+1}^p \cap Z_{r-1}^{p+1} = F^{p+1} \cap d^{-1}(F^{p+r+1}) = Z_r^{p+1}\). So \(H(E_r)^p \cong Z_{r+1}^p/(B_{r+1}^p + Z_r^{p+1}) = E_{r+1}^p\).

Convergence. Fix \(n\) and \(p\) and let \(Z\), \(B\) denote the cocycles and coboundaries of \(K\). If the filtration is finite in degrees \(n - 1\), \(n\) and \(n + 1\), then for large \(r\) we have \(F^{p+r}K^{n+1} = 0\) and \(F^{p-r+1}K^{n-1} = K^{n-1}\), so in degree \(n\): \(Z_r^p = F^p \cap Z\), \(Z_{r-1}^{p+1} = F^{p+1} \cap Z\) and \(B_r^p = F^p \cap B\). Hence \(E_\infty ^p = (F^p \cap Z)/((F^p \cap B) + (F^{p+1} \cap Z))\). On the other hand \(F^pH^n = (F^p \cap Z + B)/B\), and

\[ \frac{F^pH^n}{F^{p+1}H^n} \cong \frac{F^p \cap Z}{(F^p \cap Z) \cap (F^{p+1} \cap Z + B)} = \frac{F^p \cap Z}{F^{p+1} \cap Z + F^p \cap B}, \]

using the modular law. For the last statement, \(\mathrm{gr}^pK = 0\) for \(p {\lt} 0\) and \(\mathrm{gr}^pK^n = 0\) for \(p {\gt} n\), so \(E_1^{p,q} = 0\) unless \(p \geq 0\) and \(q \geq 0\), and \(F^pH^n\) runs from \(H^n\) to \(0\).
