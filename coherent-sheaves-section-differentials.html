52.6 Sheaves of differentials01MR

Complete. This chapter is written; its proofs await independent checking.

01MS Definition 52.6.1

Let \(f \colon X \to Y\) be a morphism of schemes. The sheaf of relative differentials \(\Omega _{X/Y}\) is the quasi-coherent \(\mathcal{O}_X\)-module obtained by gluing the sheaves \((\Omega _{B/A})^\sim \) on affine opens \(\operatorname {Spec}B \subseteq f^{-1}(\operatorname {Spec}A)\), together with the derivation \(d \colon \mathcal{O}_X \to \Omega _{X/Y}\). The gluing is possible because \(\Omega \) commutes with localisation (Proposition 13.12.2(5)). For \(X\) smooth of relative dimension \(n\) over \(Y\), the canonical sheaf is \(\omega _{X/Y} = \Lambda ^n\Omega _{X/Y}\), and the tangent sheaf is \(\mathcal{T}_{X/Y} = \Omega _{X/Y}^\vee \).

01MT Proposition 52.6.2

  1. For morphisms \(X \to Y \to Z\) there is an exact sequence \(f^*\Omega _{Y/Z} \to \Omega _{X/Z} \to \Omega _{X/Y} \to 0\).

  2. For a closed subscheme \(Y \subseteq X\) over \(Z\) with ideal sheaf \(\mathcal{I}\) there is an exact sequence \(\mathcal{I}/\mathcal{I}^2 \to \Omega _{X/Z}|_Y \to \Omega _{Y/Z} \to 0\) (the conormal sequence).

  3. \(\Omega \) commutes with base change: \(\Omega _{X_{Y'}/Y'} \cong g'^*\Omega _{X/Y}\).

  4. If \(X \to Y\) is smooth of relative dimension \(n\), then \(\Omega _{X/Y}\) is locally free of rank \(n\). In particular, a nonsingular variety of dimension \(n\) over an algebraically closed field has \(\Omega _{X/k}\) locally free of rank \(n\).

Proof â–¼

(1), (2) are Proposition 13.12.2(3), (4) on affine opens. (3) holds because \(\Omega _{(B \otimes _AA')/A'} \cong \Omega _{B/A} \otimes _AA'\), which follows from the universal property. (4) Locally \(B = A[x_1, \ldots , x_{n+r}]/(g_1, \ldots , g_r)\) with \(\det (\partial g_i/\partial x_{n+j})\) invertible. By Proposition 13.12.2(2), (4), \(\Omega _{B/A}\) is the quotient of the free module on \(dx_1, \ldots , dx_{n+r}\) by the relations \(dg_i = \sum _j(\partial g_i/\partial x_j)dx_j = 0\). Since the \(r \times r\) block for \(dx_{n+1}, \ldots , dx_{n+r}\) is invertible, these relations express \(dx_{n+1}, \ldots , dx_{n+r}\) uniquely in terms of \(dx_1, \ldots , dx_n\), and \(\Omega _{B/A}\) is free on \(dx_1, \ldots , dx_n\). The last statement uses Proposition 51.8.5.

01MU Theorem 52.6.3 Euler sequence

On \(X = \mathbb {P}^r_A\) there is an exact sequence of locally free sheaves

\[ 0 \to \Omega _{X/A} \to \mathcal{O}(-1)^{r+1} \to \mathcal{O}_X \to 0, \]

where the second map sends the basis section \(e_j\) to \(x_j\). Consequently \(\omega _{\mathbb {P}^r_A/A} \cong \mathcal{O}(-r-1)\).

Proof â–¼

Write \(\mathcal{O}(-1)^{r+1} = (S(-1)^{r+1})^\sim \) with basis \(e_0, \ldots , e_r\) of degree \(1\), and let \(\pi \) be the map \(e_j \mapsto x_j\). It is surjective: on \(U_i\), the section \(e_i/x_i\) maps to \(1\). On \(U_i = \operatorname {Spec}A[x_0/x_i, \ldots , x_r/x_i]\), \(\Omega \) is free on the \(d(x_j/x_i)\), \(j \neq i\). Define

\[ \varphi _i \colon \Omega |_{U_i} \to \mathcal{O}(-1)^{r+1}|_{U_i}, \qquad d(x_j/x_i) \mapsto \frac{1}{x_i^2}\left(x_ie_j - x_je_i\right) . \]

The composite with \(\pi \) is \(0\). The kernel of \(\pi \) on \(U_i\) consists of the \(\sum _ja_je_j\) (\(a_j\) of degree \(-1\)) with \(\sum _ja_jx_j = 0\), that is, \(a_i = -\sum _{j \neq i}a_jx_j/x_i\), and then \(\sum _ja_je_j = \sum _{j \neq i}(a_jx_i)\, \varphi _i(d(x_j/x_i))\) with \(a_jx_i\) of degree \(0\). The images \(\varphi _i(d(x_j/x_i))\) are independent, since the coefficient of \(e_j\) is \(1/x_i\). So \(\varphi _i\) is an isomorphism onto \(\ker \pi |_{U_i}\). On \(U_i \cap U_k\), writing \(x_j/x_i = (x_j/x_k)(x_k/x_i)\) and \(d(x_k/x_i) = -(x_k/x_i)^2d(x_i/x_k)\) shows \(\varphi _k(d(x_j/x_i)) = \varphi _i(d(x_j/x_i))\) by a direct computation. So the \(\varphi _i\) glue. Taking determinants in the exact sequence gives \(\omega \otimes \mathcal{O} \cong \det (\mathcal{O}(-1)^{r+1}) = \mathcal{O}(-r-1)\).

01MV Proposition 52.6.4 Adjunction

Let \(Y \subseteq X\) be nonsingular varieties over an algebraically closed field, of dimensions \(n - 1\) and \(n\), with ideal sheaf \(\mathcal{I}\). Then \(\mathcal{I}\) is invertible, \(\mathcal{I}/\mathcal{I}^2 \cong \mathcal{I}|_Y\) is the conormal sheaf, the conormal sequence \(0 \to \mathcal{I}/\mathcal{I}^2 \to \Omega _X|_Y \to \Omega _Y \to 0\) is exact on the left, and

\[ \omega _Y \cong \omega _X \otimes \mathcal{I}^{-1}|_Y . \]

For a nonsingular hypersurface \(Y\) of degree \(d\) in \(\mathbb {P}^n\), \(\omega _Y \cong \mathcal{O}_Y(d - n - 1)\).

Proof â–¼

Near a point of \(Y\), \(\mathcal{O}_X\) is a regular local ring, hence a unique factorisation domain (Remark 13.11.3), and the height-one prime of \(Y\) is principal, generated by \(g\). So \(\mathcal{I}\) is invertible and \(\mathcal{I}/\mathcal{I}^2 = \mathcal{I} \otimes \mathcal{O}_Y\). The map \(\mathcal{I}/\mathcal{I}^2 \to \Omega _X|_Y\) sends \(g\) to \(dg\). Both sides of the right-exact conormal sequence are locally free, of ranks \(1\), \(n\) and \(n - 1\) (Proposition 52.6.2(4)). A surjection of locally free sheaves of ranks \(n\) and \(n - 1\) has locally free kernel of rank \(1\), which is the image of \(\mathcal{I}/\mathcal{I}^2\). A surjection between invertible sheaves is an isomorphism, since locally it is multiplication by a function that generates the unit ideal. So the sequence is exact. Taking determinants gives \(\omega _X|_Y \cong \omega _Y \otimes \mathcal{I}/\mathcal{I}^2\). For a hypersurface of degree \(d\), \(\mathcal{I} = \mathcal{O}(-d)\) and \(\omega _{\mathbb {P}^n} = \mathcal{O}(-n-1)\).
